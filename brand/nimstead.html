Brand Identity System

Nimstead

An independent company whose work is in plain sight. Heavy, certain type, lots of white space, and one crimson seal used only where something has been checked. The aim is simple: you know we know what we're doing, you know what you're getting, and you know we're on your side.

01 — Logo

The wordmark and the seal.

Nimstead in Archivo ExtraBold, tracked tight, followed by a small crimson square: the seal. The seal comes from the original "proof point" in the product mark. It means checked, and it's the only place the logo uses colour. This is a starter typographic wordmark, not a finished logo. A type designer should redraw it before launch.

02 — Color

Black, white, and one seal.

The brand is ink on paper. Crimson is rationed. It marks the seal, a single highlighted word, and the "Fix now" state, and that's it. If crimson appears more than once or twice on a screen, it has stopped meaning anything.

Colour don'ts
  • No crimson text on Ink: it fails contrast (2.67). On dark grounds, crimson is only a fill, like the seal or a highlight behind white text.
  • Never use crimson as a background for whole panels or buttons in sequence. One accent button per screen at most.
  • Don't add blue, teal or coral, even as status colours. They pull Nimstead toward the rest of the category (and toward Nexthink in particular).
  • Don't show status with colour alone. Every state has a word ("Fix now", "Verified"); colour only backs it up.
03 — Typography

Archivo, one family.

Archivo by Omnibus-Type (Héctor Gatti), free on Google Fonts. It's a grotesque that holds up at 136px and at 12px, so one family does everything. Heavy for statements, regular for reading. Body text is never bold.

06 — Voice & Tone

Say what happened. Show the proof.

We write for IT and security leads who are busy and have been oversold before. Short sentences. Real numbers. No fear-selling.

Competent

We know what we're doing, so we show it with specifics, not adjectives.

Do"Upgrading openssl fixes 12 vulnerabilities on 3 hosts. Two are on the CISA KEV list."
Don't"Our cutting-edge AI-powered platform delivers next-gen vulnerability intelligence."

Predictable

You know what you're getting. We say what will happen before it happens, and confirm it after.

Do"We'll update your 2 canary machines first. If a service fails its health check, we roll back automatically."
Don't"Sit back and relax. We'll take care of everything!"

On your side

Independent and frank. We tell you what matters and what can wait, even when the answer is "nothing to do".

Do"Nothing needs fixing today. Your evidence pack for ISO 27001 A.8.8 is up to date."
Don't"⚠ 4,212 CRITICAL THREATS DETECTED. Act now before it's too late."
