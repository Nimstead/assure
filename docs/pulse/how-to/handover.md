# Prepare an analyst handover

Prepare plain text for another analyst after you analyse your CVE list. The handover covers the full analysed list, independent of table filters.

1. Check the coverage counts and source dates.
2. Review snapshot comparison results, if you loaded a baseline.
3. Select **Copy handover** or **Download handover**.
4. Read the resulting text before sharing it.
5. Add the environment-specific checks and ownership information your colleague needs.

The text contains a priority queue, missing or rejected IDs, source dates, available comparisons, and checks for the next analyst.

Keep the handover private. It contains your selected CVE list. Clearing Pulse does not remove downloaded files or clipboard copies.

Do not treat a federal deadline as your organisation's remediation status. Confirm affected products, asset exposure, and completed fixes separately.

Source: [Pulse repository documentation](https://github.com/Nimstead/pulse/blob/main/README.md).
